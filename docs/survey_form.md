# Blind listening survey form

Create one Google Form section per clip. Keep the clip IDs neutral (`Clip A` through `Clip F`) and shuffle the six sections or use multiple randomized form versions.

For every clip, require these two questions:

1. **Human or machine?** — Human / Machine
2. **How musical is this clip?** — Linear scale from 1 (not musical) to 5 (highly musical)

Use the six files listed in `docs/survey_clips_manifest.md`. Do not reveal the source mapping to participants. The target is at least 20 complete responses.
